POWER RAILS:
VBAT_RAW : LiPo (+) to Switch Pin 1
VBAT_SW : Switch Output -> MP1584 IN+ & DRV8833 VM
5V : Buck OUT+ -> ESP32 Pin 21 (5V)
3V3 : ESP32 Pin 1 -> QTR VCC, DRV nSLEEP, Encoders
GND : All module grounds

MOTOR DRIVE OUTPUTS:
MOT_L_P : DRV8833 OUT1 -> Left Motor JST Pin 1
MOT_L_N : DRV8833 OUT2 -> Left Motor JST Pin 2
MOT_R_P : DRV8833 OUT3 -> Right Motor JST Pin 1
MOT_R_N : DRV8833 OUT4 -> Right Motor JST Pin 2

DRV8833 PWM INPUTS:
M_L_IN1 : ESP32 GPIO 12 -> DRV8833 IN1
M_L_IN2 : ESP32 GPIO 13 -> DRV8833 IN2
M_R_IN3 : ESP32 GPIO 14 -> DRV8833 IN3
M_R_IN4 : ESP32 GPIO 15 -> DRV8833 IN4

ENCODER PCNT:
ENC_L_A : Left Enc Pin 5 -> GPIO 17
ENC_L_B : Left Enc Pin 6 -> GPIO 18
ENC_R_A : Right Enc Pin 5 -> GPIO 21
ENC_R_B : Right Enc Pin 6 -> GPIO 38

QTR-8A SENSORS:
QTR_1 to QTR_8 -> Sensors 1 to 8 -> GPIO 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8
SAFETY RULE: NEVER FEED 5V TO QTR-8A
Powering QTR-8A with 5V outputs 0–5V signals into ESP32 ADC pins, causing permanent pin gate breakdown. 3.3V keeps sensor outputs strictly within the safe 0–3.3V range.
BUCK CONVERTER TUNING:
Do NOT connect the ESP32 to the MP1584 until you attach the 2S battery and turn the potentiometer screw with a multimeter until it reads exactly 5.00V DC.
COPPER TRACE WIDTHS:
* Motor Rails (VBAT_RAW, VBAT_SW, VM, MOT_x): 1.2mm - 1.5mm
* Digital/ADC/PWM: 0.254mm (10 mil)
* Solder the 100uF capacitor as close as possible to DRV8833 VM & GND pins.
